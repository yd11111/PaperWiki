- [[论文笔记/StreamMel|StreamMel]] — 2025, Nankai+Microsoft, 首个单阶段连续 mel 流式零样本 TTS, text-mel interleaving (1:4), FPL-A 0.01s, cross-sentence WER-H 2.76 / MOS 4.14

- [[论文笔记/CTDiffusion|CTDiffusion]] — 2025, MLLM 中逐帧 continuous-token diffusion head + dual-head 架构 + two-stage 训练, 160M 参数 SOTA AR zero-shot TTS
- [[论文笔记/BridgeCode|BridgeCode]] — 2025, 双表征范式 (10 Hz sparse tokens + 50 Hz dense continuous features) + 双向 bridging module, AR TTS 低帧率高质量
- [[论文笔记/UniVoice|UniVoice]] — 2025, 单 LLM + dual attention mask 统一 AR-ASR 和 flow-matching-TTS, 连续表征框架
- [[论文笔记/TTS-Transducer|TTS-Transducer]] — 2025, NVIDIA, RNNT transducer 预测第一码本 + NAR Transformer 预测残余码本, 端到端联合训练, codec-agnostic, CER 3.94% challenging texts (best robustness)
- [[论文笔记/SMLLE|SMLLE]] — 2025, Microsoft+SJTU, 首个逐帧流式零样本 TTS, Transducer→semantic tokens + MELLE-based AR→mel, Delete <Bos> Mechanism, WER-C 5.14% streaming / 1.03% R5

## Codec 与 Tokenizer 设计

### 2025
- [[论文笔记/TaDiCodec|TaDiCodec]] — 2025, CUHK-SZ, 端到端 diffusion autoencoder speech tokenizer (6.25Hz, 0.0875kbps) + AR/MGM TTS, WER 2.28 (en) / 1.19 (zh) on SeedTTS test, RTF 0.12-0.29
- [[论文笔记/MSR-Codec|MSR-Codec]] — 2025, LIGHTSPEED+HKUST, 级联残差四流 codec (semantic/timbre/prosody/residual) + 0.2B 两阶段 AR TTS, 隐式解耦无需对抗训练, 45k h 训练达 WER 3.07 / SIM 0.613 / RTF 0.67 (Seed-TTS-eval), 效率-性能权衡突出
